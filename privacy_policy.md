# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 June 2026  

**App name:** Catat Keuangan  
**Package name:** com.developerzero.apps.moneytracker  
**Developer:** Developer Zero (Emmanuel)

## 1. Overview
Catat Keuangan is a personal finance‑tracking app that helps you record income, expenses, and generate simple reports. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you provide.

## 2. Information We Collect

| Category | Collected Data | Purpose |
|----------|----------------|---------|
| **Account** | Email address (via Google Sign‑In) | Identify the user, keep data isolated per account, enable cloud sync (if any). |
| **Transactions** | Date, amount, category, description, optional receipt image (stored locally). | Provide core app functionality – tracking and reporting of finances. |
| **Device Info** | OS version, device model, app version, crash logs (opt‑in). | Diagnose issues, improve stability, and enhance performance. |
| **Analytics (optional)** | Usage events (screen views, button taps) – only if you enable the optional analytics flag in the app. | Understand how the app is used to prioritize improvements. |

**No** collection of location, contacts, microphone, camera (except for optional receipt images you explicitly add), or any other sensitive personal data.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- **Store locally** on your device; data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export it (XLS/PDF) or use Google Sign‑In for cloud‑sync (in which case the data is stored in your Google account’s encrypted storage).
- **Exported files** (XLS, PDF) are saved on the device’s file system; you control when and where to share them.
- **Crash logs** are sent anonymously to Expo’s error‑reporting service to help us fix bugs.

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure
We **do not sell, trade, or rent** your personal information to third parties. The only data transmitted off‑device is:
- **Google Sign‑In token** – used solely for authentication with Google and never stored by us.
- **Crash/Analytics reports** – sent to Expo’s secure servers under their privacy terms.

## 5. Data Retention
All data remains on your device until you delete it manually (individual transactions, exported files, or the whole app). Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

## 6. Your Rights
- **Access & modify** your transaction data anytime within the app.
- **Delete** any transaction or the entire data set via the UI.
- **Revoke** Google Sign‑In access from your Google account settings at any time.

## 7. Security
- Data is stored in encrypted local storage (via `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`).
- Communication with Google’s OAuth endpoint uses HTTPS.
- We follow industry‑standard best practices for secure coding and dependency management.

## 8. Children’s Privacy
The app is **not directed to children under 13** and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone in that age group.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect legal or functional changes. The effective date above will be revised, and a notice will be displayed within the app.
